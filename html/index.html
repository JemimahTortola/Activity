Game Tips & Guides I've Explored

Here are some tips from my favorite games that I've learned while playing. Click each card to read the full guide!

Endfield Factory Tips in Valley 4

Focus on hyper-optimized blueprints to maximize purple battery and explosives output without wasting ore. Place transport belts in a compact loop, prioritize Cryston Components early, and use recycling stations to reclaim excess materials. My favorite setup gets 6/min HC Valley Batteries with minimal idle machines!

Wuthering Waves Beginner & Combat Tips

Master parries and dodges to build Concerto energy fast, equip the right Echo set for your main DPS, and always explore for Sonance Caskets. Save Astrite for limited banners instead of standard pulls, and don't sleep on Uncle Wei's weapon crafting for early upgrades!

Limbus Company Team Building & Clash Tips

Build teams around sinners affinities for better clash wins, uptie Identities to level 3 as soon as possible, and learn to read enemy skill coins to avoid brutal losses. Prioritize pulling ID's that fit for your team comp, and grind the dungeon for shards to upgrade your sinners!

Honkai Star Rail Beginner & Team Tips

Focus on building one main DPS + sustain + support early. Save Stellar Jades for limited banners, explore every map for chests, and prioritize Relics farming for your core team. Don't spread resources too thin; level 5-6 characters max at first!
